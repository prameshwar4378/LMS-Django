Our Mission & Story

Re-imagining Hospitality Operations
Beyond Expectations.

InnVetrix is built by Ultoxy Technologies with a single mission: to provide independent hoteliers and lodge owners with modern, enterprise-grade cloud automation that eliminates cashier leakage and accelerates guest service.

Why We Built InnVetrix

The Problem With Traditional Lodge Software

For decades, independent lodges, heritage guest houses, and boutique hotels have been trapped between two bad options: archaic, clunky desktop software from the 1990s that crashes frequently, or exorbitantly expensive enterprise hotel software requiring complicated servers and massive upfront licensing fees.

Meanwhile, reception desks continued losing thousands of rupees each week in unchecked cash drawer discrepancies, delayed billing math mistakes, and lost room service orders.

Our Core Commitment: "We believe technology should be an asset, not an obstacle. InnVetrix was engineered to deliver bank-grade financial discipline, sub-minute check-ins, and delightful digital room catalogues without hardware friction."

The Ultoxy Technologies Standard

Financial Integrity First

Our shift cashier drawer audits and discrepancy lockouts permanently protect property revenue from internal theft.

Cloud Reliability & Speed

Zero installation required. Access your reception desk and live occupancy from any laptop, tablet, or phone 24/7.
