MODULE 03 • FINANCIAL INTEGRITY & CASH CONTROL

Anti-Theft Shift Cashier & Drawer Lockout Audit

Cash leakage during shift handovers is the #1 silent profit drain in the lodge business. InnVetrix enforces iron-clad financial discipline between reception cashiers.

  • Mandatory Opening Float: Cashier cannot initiate check-ins or accept guest money without recording verified physical cash in the drawer.
  • Multi-Mode Split Ledger: Automatically segregates Cash, UPI QR, Card Terminal, and Bank NEFT payments into separate auditable buckets.
  • Discrepancy Lockout & Handover Notes: If physical cash does not match system collections down to the rupee, the shift cannot be closed without an explanatory note and manager override.
  • Petty Cash Paid-Out Vouchers: Every tea, laundry, or maintenance payout requires recorded reason, recipient name, and instant deduction from cash expected.
