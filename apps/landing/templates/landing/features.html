Module 03

Anti-Theft Shift Cashier & Drawer Lockout Audit

Cash leakage during shift changes is the #1 silent profit drain in the lodge business. InnVetrix enforces iron-clad financial discipline between reception staff.

  • Mandatory Opening Float: Cashier must verify opening cash before processing any customer transactions.
  • Multi-Mode Split Reconciliation: Every transaction is categorized by Cash, UPI QR, Card, or Bank NEFT.
  • Discrepancy Lockout & Handover Note: If physical cash does not match system collections, cashier cannot close drawer without manager override and explanatory note.
  • Owner Audit Log: Every paid-out expense or petty cash withdrawal requires recipient signature and reason.
