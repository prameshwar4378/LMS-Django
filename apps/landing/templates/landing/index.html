Every module in InnVetrix is designed with input from veteran lodge owners and front-office cashiers to solve everyday bottlenecks.

Front-Desk & Stay Lifecycle

Instant guest check-in with ID photo upload, room key assignment, stay extensions, and 1-click express check-outs.

  • Express check-in under 45 seconds
  • Government ID proof capture (Aadhaar/Passport)
  • Stay history & repeat guest recognition

Interactive Room Tape Chart

A visual, high-speed multi-room calendar. Drag-and-drop bookings, view occupancy rates, and spot room maintenance states at a glance.

  • Real-time color-coded room status grid
  • Prevent double-booking permanently
  • Housekeeping clean/dirty status tracker

Anti-Theft Shift Cashier Audit

Lockdown your cash drawer. Every shift requires opening float declaration, records all cash in/out, and mandates handover verification.

  • Zero untracked cash leakage
  • Shift handover notes & manager sign-off
  • Audit trail of every payment mode (Cash/UPI/Card)

Dynamic Guest QR Catalogue

Place branded QR standees in rooms. Guests scan with their smartphones to view dynamic food menus, hotel amenities, photo galleries, and local transit.

  • 100% dynamic — manage from dashboard
  • Print-ready A4 factsheet & PDF generator
  • Zero app installation needed by guests

Split Invoicing & Thermal Printing

Generate compliant GST invoices in seconds. Support for split billing between roommates, corporate GST billing, and instant 80mm thermal receipt roll printing.

  • 80mm ESC/POS thermal receipt printing
  • Detailed A4 GST tax invoices with HSN codes
  • Advance deposit adjustments & refund tracking

Multi-Branch Chain Controls

Manage multiple lodges, hotels, or resort branches from a single owner login. Switch properties instantly with centralized financial intelligence.

  • Multi-tenant isolation for security
  • Role-based permissions (Cashier, Manager, Owner)
  • Consolidated executive revenue reports
